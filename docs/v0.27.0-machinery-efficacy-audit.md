# v0.27.0 — Machinery Efficacy Audit (dormant-check pruning)

Status: **Phase 2 complete (evidence gathered) — awaiting per-unit greenlight
(Phase 3).** No skill files changed yet. Informs the v0.27.0 cycle.

Applies the author's v0.10.0 methodology ("0 true catches → hold") to the whole
gate-check catalog + non-check machinery, using `/Users/n8/git/graph` fire
history (285 sprints). Distinct from retro Step-4's **structural** dormancy scans
(wiring/anchor liveness) — this measures **efficacy** (did a unit ever catch a
real defect) against **cost** (resident tokens per gate). Reproduce with
`node scripts/audit-machinery-efficacy.js --graph <consumer>` (run under `bun`
for the real tiktoken count; plain `node` falls back to chars/4).

## Method & caveats (read before trusting a number)

1. **Verdict tokens are PASS-dominated.** A naive `grep FAIL` over gate logs
   over-counts on check *descriptions* ("Check 2 (no unresolved HARD_BLOCK)").
   Parsed against the real `- Check N (...): VERDICT` grammar, 706 clean verdicts
   gave **0 FAILED, 3 DEFERRED**. So "logged no FAIL" is NOT a dormancy signal.
   The catch signals used instead: escalation-archive `Check N` references
   (`escRef`), retro-corpus `Check N` citations (`retroRef`), and non-PASS
   verdicts (`nonPASS`, weak).
2. **Number-drift → align by title — and title-align UNDER-attributes.**
   Consumer check numbering has forked from the distribution's for high IDs
   (consumer C21="validation intensity" = distribution Check 20). The audit
   aligns by stemmed word-Jaccard on titles, not by number. **Critical
   limitation proven during investigation:** a `—` in `align→` does NOT mean
   dormant — it can mean the consumer runs the check under a *different title
   and number*. Two of the three top "no-trace" flags turned out live:
   distribution **Check 21 ← graph Check 33** (self-documented in the check's own
   `Graph→distribution number mapping` note) and distribution **Check 19's
   core-path-wiring clause ↔ consumer C30 "orphaned-fn/wiring"** (fired s275).
   The automated signal is a *lead*, not a verdict; every candidate must be read
   before any removal (this is why the plan gates removal behind investigation).
3. **Token basis:** tiktoken `cl100k` proxy (~15% off Claude's tokenizer but
   consistent and far better than chars/4). Rankings are basis-invariant.
4. **H1/H2 are distribution-side guards.** The self-test fixtures they audit
   (`core/fixtures/*`) are **not installed in the consumer** (confirmed: no
   fixtures dir in graph). H1/H2 run in the distribution's own CI self-test, so
   consumer fire-history structurally **under-measures** them — do not read their
   0 consumer catches as dormancy.

## DECISIVE CORRECTION — the consumer runs a SEPARATE, opted-out catalog

Reading the consumer's actual check definitions overturned the "absorption gap"
framing. The consumer's high-numbered + several redefined checks live in
`.claude/skills/ai-dlc/extensions/checks/gate-validation-domain.md`, whose
frontmatter declares **`push_candidate: false`**. This is a full **domain
catalog** layered via Rule-27 extensions that:

- **Redefines shared numbers** with graph-DeFi content: consumer Check 20 =
  "HPE ADR probe-evidence" (distribution Check 20 = validation-intensity);
  consumer Check 22 = "smoke test evidence" (distribution Check 22 =
  teammate-spawn role binding); consumer Check 15/17/H1 are domain-extended.
- **Adds consumer-only checks** 19b, 23–33 (deploy-freshness, financial-display
  ground-truth, post-merge fail-closed audit, deployed-ranges, config-integrity,
  execution-health, merge-approval, orphaned-fn, cited-SHA laundering, …), all
  tied to `rebalancer/**`, `audit-main-since.sh`, subgraph/on-chain domains.

**Two consequences:**
1. **Per-number fire attribution is confounded** for every redefined number, not
   just the high ones — graph's retro/escalation `Check N` references cite the
   *consumer's* catalog. The efficacy table's escRef/retroRef counts conflate
   two catalogs and must NOT be read as distribution-check dormancy evidence.
2. **The "backport C25" recommendation is RETRACTED.** C25 (and its siblings) are
   `push_candidate: false` by the consumer's own declaration — deliberately
   consumer-local domain hardening, correctly placed in the extensions layer.
   Backporting would violate the layered-rulebook design (Rule 27). There is no
   absorption gap here; there is a correctly-drawn core/extension boundary.

**What the audit CAN soundly conclude:** the distribution's **core** checks 1–18
map cleanly to the consumer's core usage; the consumer's domain catalog is a
separate, intentionally-non-pushed layer; and distribution-check dormancy CANNOT
be inferred from this consumer's logs (its fire-history is against its own
catalog). A real distribution-check dormancy signal would need either the
distribution's own CI self-test history or a *non-domain* consumer.
